# Provenance and Attribution

The project grew out of a user-led reconstruction exercise involving inaccessible ChatGPT conversation content and local Firefox SessionStore artifacts. The current protocol formalizes access-boundary assessment, preservation, extraction, cross-source correlation, timeline reconstruction, negative-evidence evaluation and reporting.

## Earlier timeline-reconstruction work (user-reported)
The contributor reports having developed and applied timeline-reconstruction methods in Hungary in 2009–2010 in criminal investigations, later in cybercrime and economic-protection work. This is contributor-provided historical information and is not independently verified by documents in this repository.

CMNR must not be represented as the invention of timeline reconstruction. It is a later formalization and extension for a specific digital-trace reconstruction task class. Novelty and priority have not been established by a completed prior-art review.

Preserve attribution; distinguish historical methods from later implementation; do not claim joint invention where earlier independent work is reported; record future contributions and dates in commit history.
