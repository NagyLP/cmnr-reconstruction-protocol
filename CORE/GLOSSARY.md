# CMNR Controlled Glossary

- **Trace carrier (nyomhordozó):** physical or digital medium in which a trace is recorded.
- **Digital trace (digitális nyom):** data produced by an activity or technical process and available for examination.
- **Trace fragment (nyomtöredék):** incomplete surviving trace.
- **Alteration/change (elváltozás/változás):** difference from an earlier state.
- **Data remnant (adatmaradvány):** data retained from an earlier process.
- **Derived data (származtatott adat):** value produced by analysis and not present in that form in the source.
- **Artifact (artefaktum):** broad technical term; use more precise terms above where possible.
- **Access boundary:** what the analyst can and cannot retrieve.
- **Negative evidence:** absence of an expected trace, evidential only under validated assumptions.
- **Correlation:** documented relationship; not automatically causation.

Do not confuse a trace, its carrier, a fragment, a change, and an analyst-derived value.
